Locked derivation method:
1. Take the United Kingdom `Index` history through December 2024.
2. Apply a 12-month moving average.
3. Take natural logs of the smoothed series.
4. Remove a linear trend with ordinary least squares.
5. Compute the FFT power spectrum.
6. Search only periods from 60 to 240 months.
7. Take the period at the dominant admissible FFT peak.
