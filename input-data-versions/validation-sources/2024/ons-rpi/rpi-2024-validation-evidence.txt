Important definition note:
- RPI means Rental Price Index in this validation metric.
- It does not mean Retail Prices Index.

Table and columns used:
- Workbook `Table 1`
- Geography: Great Britain
- Months: 2024-01 through 2024-12

Geography note:
- The workbook marks the UK series as unavailable for the 2011 target year.
- The validation target therefore uses Great Britain for both 2011 and 2024 to keep the 2011 overlay and 2024 catalog comparable.

Transformations:
- Read the Great Britain monthly rental price index for 2024.
- Rebase monthly values to January 2024 = 1.0.
- rpi_mean is the arithmetic mean of the 12 rebased monthly values.
