# 2011 buy-to-let rental-yield validation evidence
#
# Author: Max Stoddard

Purpose:
- Public evidence bundle for the 2011 UK average gross buy-to-let rental yield
  used by the v0-only 2011 validation profile.

Tracked public references:
- PropertyWire, `Healthy demand for rental property pushes up yields across the UK`
  URL: https://www.propertywire.com/news/europe/uk-buy-let-yields/
  Published: 27 March 2012
  Extracted value:
  - 2011 UK average gross rental yield: `6.1%`
- PropertyWire, `Rental yields edge higher for buy to let landlords`
  URL: https://www.propertywire.com/news/europe/uk-landlords-rental-yields/
  Published: 10 August 2012
  Cross-check context:
  - June 2011 UK average gross rental yield: `6.0%`
- PropertyWire, `Complex buy to let yields reach new UK high`
  URL: https://www.propertywire.com/news/europe/uk-buy-let-yields-2/
  Published: 17 April 2012
  Cross-check context:
  - Q4 2011 mainstream vanilla buy-to-let yield: `6.1%`

Published target:
- `core_rentalYield` source value: `6.1`
- Target band: `+/-15%` => `5.185` to `7.015`

Notes:
- The 2024 rental-yield metric uses UK Finance quarterly values and the observed
  quarterly range. The 2011 public archive does not expose an equally clean UK
  quarterly series, so the v0-only 2011 profile locks to the BM Solutions annual
  UK yield with a fixed `+/-15%` tolerance.
