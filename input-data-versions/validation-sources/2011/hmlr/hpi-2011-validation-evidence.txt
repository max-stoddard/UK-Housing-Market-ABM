# 2011 HPI validation evidence
#
# Author: Max Stoddard

Derived from:
- input-data-versions/validation-sources/2024/hmlr/UK-HPI-full-file-2024-12.csv

Tracked 2011 snapshots:
- input-data-versions/validation-sources/2011/hmlr/uk-hpi-uk-series-through-2011-12.csv
- input-data-versions/validation-sources/2011/hmlr/hpi-2011-derived-values.json

Method:
- Filter the archived full-file CSV down to the United Kingdom rows through
  `2011-12`.
- Reuse the existing HPI helper methodology that the 2024 profile already locks:
  rebased mean, rebased population std, and dominant cycle period from the
  filtered UK series.

Derived values:
- `core_hpiMean`: UK `IndexSA` from `2011-01` to `2011-12`, rebased to January
  2011 = `1.0`, annual mean `0.9922839506172839`, target band `+/-15%`.
- `core_hpiStd`: UK `IndexSA` from `2005-01` to `2011-12`, rebased to January
  2005 = `1.0`, population std `0.06520815312915954`, target band `+/-15%`.
- `core_hpiCyclePeriod`: UK `Index` history through `2011-12`, 12-month moving
  average, log transform, linear detrend, FFT peak search over `60..240` months,
  dominant cycle period `171.33333333333334`, target band `+/-15%`.

Notes:
- The source publication date is later than 2011, but the archived HMLR full-file
  release contains the explicit historical UK series required to reconstruct the
  2011 targets reproducibly.
