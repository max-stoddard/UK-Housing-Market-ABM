# 2011 BoE housing-tools validation evidence
#
# Author: Max Stoddard

Derived from:
- input-data-versions/validation-sources/2024/boe/housing-tools.xlsx

Tracked 2011 snapshot:
- input-data-versions/validation-sources/2011/boe/housing-tools-2011-series.csv

Method:
- Extract the 2011 rows from the long-run Bank of England housing-tools workbook.
- Keep the locked workbook sheet semantics used by the 2024 validation catalog.
- Publish the extracted 2011 rows as a minimal repo-local CSV so the v0 2011
  profile does not depend on the larger 2024 workbook path.

Metric mappings:
- `core_mortgageApprovals`: sheet `4.Mortgage approvals`, 2011 monthly series.
  Source value is the 2011 annual mean converted to thousand count/month:
  `49.28633333333333`. Target band is the observed 2011 monthly range:
  `42.883` to `52.93`.
- `core_housingTransactions`: sheet `5.Housing transactions`, 2011 monthly series.
  Source value is the 2011 annual mean converted to thousand count/month:
  `73.55166666666666`. Target band is the observed 2011 monthly range:
  `68.68` to `77.16`.
- `core_debtToIncome`: sheet `3. Household debt to income`, 2011 quarterly series.
  Source value is the 2011 annual mean: `162.93794051498202`. Target band is the
  observed 2011 quarterly range: `161.8704279215477` to `164.2259943729304`.
- `core_priceToIncome`: sheet `7.House prices disp. income`, 2011 quarterly
  series. Source value is the 2011 annual mean: `4.240062566676624`. Target band
  is the observed 2011 quarterly range: `4.225101341813285` to
  `4.268301630303742`.
- `core_housePriceGrowth`: sheet `6.House price growth`, 2011 monthly series.
  Source value is the 2011 annual mean: `-0.3968031463238107`. Target band is the
  observed 2011 monthly range: `-0.9449694274597076` to `0.1702610669693572`.
- `core_interestRateSpread`: sheet `8. Spreads new mortgage lending`, 2011
  monthly series. Source value is the 2011 annual mean: `2.4800784641069633`.
  Target band uses the observed 2011 quarterly-mean range:
  `2.3975732465696207` to `2.626824733041048`.

Notes:
- The 2011 reference overlay uses annual means plus observed 2011 ranges for
  these long-run macro series because there is no 2011 FPC snapshot equivalent
  to the 2024 FPC core-indicator pack.
